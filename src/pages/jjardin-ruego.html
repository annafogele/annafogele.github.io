JJARDIN / RUEGO

JJARDIN / RUEGO

One-day group show accompanied by musical performances
Pandora Art Gallery, Berlin
June 7, 2025.

The core of the exhibition is based upon the visual and emotional language of lost-object notices—particularly those for missing animals, keys, and other personal belongings—using them as points of departure to explore absence, displacement, and the quiet melancholies embedded in everyday loss.

The exhibition featured a series of visual works, including gathered missing animal and item posters, photographies, drawings, and mixed-media pieces.

In addition to the visual works, the exhibition featured live musical performances. The gallery space was transformed into a contemplative environment where attendees could reflect on the transient nature of life and that which we hold dear.
